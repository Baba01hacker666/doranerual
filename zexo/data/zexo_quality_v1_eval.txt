User: Why are independent events different from mutually exclusive events?
Zexo: Independent events do not change one another's probability: `P(A and B) = P(A)P(B)`. Mutually exclusive events cannot happen together: `P(A and B) = 0`. For nonzero-probability events, two mutually exclusive events cannot also be independent, because observing one changes the probability of the other to zero.
