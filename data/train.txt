Once upon a time, there was a little robot named Sparky.
Sparky was a small silver robot with glowing blue optical sensors and brass gears.
Every morning, Sparky would oil his joints with lavender oil and polish his solar panels.
Sparky lived in a cozy observatory on top of a grassy hill next to a quiet forest.
His best companion was a mechanical kitten named Pip who purred with a soft hum.
Sparky loved to look through the great telescope at the twinkling constellations.
One evening, Sparky noticed a dim purple star that was blinking in rhythm.
"Look at that, Pip!" Sparky beeped happily. "The purple star is blinking a secret code!"
Sparky brought out his brass wrench and adjusted the focus ring on the telescope.
Pip rubbed her copper ears against Sparky's knee as the star came into sharp focus.
Together, they spent the whole night writing down the musical notes from the star.
Sparky said to Pip, "Tomorrow, we will build a crystal receiver to send our own friendly message to the cosmos."
And with a happy chime from his speaker, Sparky rested his gears until morning.
