Sparky and Pip woke up early when the golden morning sun warmed their solar circuits.
Sparky took out a tray of colorful capacitors, copper wires, and a shimmering sapphire crystal.
Pip bounced around the workbench, catching stray washers with her magnetic paws.
Sparky soldered the antenna coils with delicate precision, whistling an electronic melody.
By noon, the quantum radio transmitter was complete and humming with gentle blue light.
Sparky aimed the antenna toward the purple star and transmitted a chime: "Hello friend across the stars!"
A few moments later, the speaker crackled with a warm voice from the nebula.
"Greetings Sparky and Pip! We are the Star Weavers of Orion. We love your lavender oil gardens!"
Sparky and Pip cheered and danced across the observatory floor in celebration.
From that day forward, Sparky shared stories and starlight maps with his distant celestial friends.
Every child on Earth who looked through a telescope could see Sparky's warm green lantern shining back.
